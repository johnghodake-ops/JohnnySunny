About US

An EPC desk that treats generation as a deliverable, not a brochure claim.

Solar EPC is owned by John G. We run a tight engineering-procurement-construction loop: load study, string design, structure, electrical, permissions, and commissioning. You get a plant that matches the roof, the tariff, and the load — not a generic kit.

Work is scoped in writing. Generation estimates sit next to assumptions. After handover, monitoring and O&M keep the array honest.

Practice principles

  1. Survey before sizing. Shade and structure first.
  2. Bill of quantities matches the drawing, not a catalogue.
  3. Commissioning includes string-level checks and a generation baseline.
  4. Owner contact stays with John G through the project.

Services

Full-scope solar, split into work you can actually buy.

Residential rooftop

3–15 kW grid-tied systems for homes. Load matching, inverter selection, net-metering file support, and a clean rooftop layout.

Commercial & industrial

20 kW to multi-MW plants on sheds, RCC roofs, and carports. Structural vetting, LT/HT interfacing, and phased shutdowns.

Hybrid & backup

Solar with storage for unstable feeders and critical loads. Battery sizing from actual outage hours, not guesswork.

Engineering study

Shade analysis, stringing, SLD, cable schedules, and earthing. Issued as a drawing set before procurement.

Procurement

Tier-1 modules, string or central inverters, MMS, BOS. Serial tracking and factory papers stay with the project file.

O&M

Cleaning cycles, thermal scans, inverter logs, and annual generation reports against the commissioning baseline.
